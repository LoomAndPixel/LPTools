# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

LP Tools is made by Loom & Pixel, a small creative studio. This site is a catalog and documentation for our tools. There are no accounts, no forms and no comments, and we don't collect anything about you unless you email us.

## Visits

This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We don't know who visits or which pages they view. If that changes, this page will say so before it does.

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads a page, under [its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Buying a tool

Tools are sold through [Gumroad](https://lptools.gumroad.com). When you click "Get the tool" you leave this site. Gumroad handles your payment, your receipt and your download, and it follows [its own privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy). We don't see your card details.

## Email

If you write to [{{ site.support_email_display }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}), we use your message and address only to reply and help with the tool. We don't sell it, share it or add you to a mailing list.

## Images and fonts

Everything on this site is loaded from lptools.store itself. No third-party fonts, scripts or embeds are used.

## Your choices

To see or delete anything you've sent us, email us and ask.

## Questions

Email [{{ site.support_email_display }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). If anything here changes, we'll update this page.
